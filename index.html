About the Model

iPhone/iPad compatibility: open the hosted HTTPS link in Safari. Direct HTML attachments opened from Messages, Mail, or Files can be sandboxed by iOS and may not be allowed to retrieve live Sleeper data.

Gridiron Gladiators Outlook simulates the remainder of the fantasy season using current standings, remaining schedule, league scoring, current rosters, bye weeks, broad player availability assumptions, and weekly scoring uncertainty. Rosters are assumed to remain unchanged while teams are assumed to make reasonable lineup substitutions using players already rostered.

The shared version intentionally does not expose player-level projections, waiver advice, trade advice, opponent weaknesses, bench rankings, or other competitive scouting detail.

Model: Share-safe Monte Carlo approximation. Probabilities are estimates, not certainty.
